    Before you apply

    Four questions that save time and money

    Ask these about every career on your shortlist before you commit to a program.

    1. What does the workday look like?Compare patient contact, lab time, how much you're on your feet, and the schedule. Shadow someone if you can.
    2. Which degree actually qualifies you?Your degree may cover some prerequisites, but licensed roles usually need a specific professional program.
    3. Is the program accredited?Only graduates of accredited programs can sit for most certification exams. Use the directory on each career page.
    4. What does your state require?Check your state licensing board for exams, fees, and rules before you enroll.
