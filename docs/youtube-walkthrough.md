## Before you hit record

- [ ] Merge the latest pull request, then hard-refresh the site (Cmd+Shift+R).
- [ ] Record in a **fresh private/incognito window**, so the shortlist and checklist start empty.
- [ ] Close the other tabs and hide the bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B) for a clean screen.
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 125% so text is readable on phones.
- [ ] Pick light mode (sun/moon button at the top right). It usually reads better on video.
- [ ] Pick your example career in advance. Medical Laboratory Scientist is your strongest, because it's where your 25 years speak.
- [ ] Decide which state to show for salaries: `[your state]`.

## Smart Simplicity System tie-in

Say once, near the start or the end: this video is one piece of the Smart Simplicity System, making hard decisions simple, practical, and step by step.

## Guardrails for this video

- Read salary figures **from the BLS page on screen** rather than from memory, and say the source out loud.
- Don't promise admission, jobs, or pay.
- No real student or patient names or details in examples.
- If you mention a personal story, keep it true and de-identified.
